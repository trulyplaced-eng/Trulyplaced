2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to run your job search. In practice that falls into five groups.

Information you give us directly

  • Your name, email address, phone number and location or time zone.
  • Your CV or resume, and everything inside it — employment history, education, skills, certifications, and any other detail you have chosen to include.
  • Your job preferences: target roles, industries, seniority, locations, remote or on-site, and salary expectations.
  • Your work-authorisation status in the countries you want to work in, and whether you require visa sponsorship.
  • Cover letters, portfolio or GitHub links, references, and any documents you send us.

Information we create for you

  • Tailored versions of your CV produced for specific applications.
  • A record of every application we submit on your behalf: employer, role, date, and status.
  • Notes from our recruiters about your search and the responses it receives.

Account credentials

To apply on your behalf we sometimes need access to job boards and employer portals in your name. Where you give us credentials, we store them encrypted and use them only to submit applications you have authorised. You can withdraw that access at any time by changing the password and telling us.

Payment information

Payments are processed by [ insert payment processor, e.g. Stripe / Razorpay / PayPal ]. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, the amount, and the last four digits of the card. We never see or store your full card number.

Website information

When you visit trulyplaced.com we collect basic technical data: IP address, browser type, pages viewed and referring site. This is ordinary server and analytics data used to understand traffic, not to identify you personally.

A note on sensitive information. CVs often contain details that count as special category or sensitive personal data — date of birth, nationality, photographs, health or disability information, or memberships that reveal religion or political views. We do not ask for any of this. If your CV contains it, we process it only because it is already part of the document you asked us to submit. You are welcome to remove anything you would rather we did not handle, and we will work with the version you give us.

3. How we use it

  • To deliver the service — rewriting your CV, finding roles, and submitting applications in your name.
  • To communicate with you — progress updates, interview notifications, and questions about your search, normally over email or WhatsApp.
  • To take payment and keep accounting records we are required by law to retain.
  • To improve the service — understanding which roles and formats generate responses, in aggregate.
  • To meet legal obligations and to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Who we share it with

Running this service necessarily means sending your information to other people. Specifically:

  • Employers, recruiters and applicant tracking systems. This is the core of what you are paying us to do. When we submit an application, your CV and contact details go to that employer and to whatever system they use to receive applications. Once an employer has your CV, their own privacy policy governs what they do with it, and we cannot retrieve it on your behalf.
  • Job boards and professional networks such as LinkedIn, Indeed and similar platforms, where applications are submitted through them.
  • Our service providers — cloud hosting, email, document storage, scheduling and our payment processor. They are bound by contract to process your data only on our instructions.
  • Our own team — the recruiters and assistants working on your search, who are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities or advisers, where we are legally required to disclose information or need to establish or defend a legal claim.

6. Where your data is processed

Our team is based in India, so your personal information is transferred to and processed in India, and may also be processed by service providers in other countries.

If you are in the UK or the EEA, this means your data is transferred outside those territories. We rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers, together with the security measures described below. You may request a copy of the relevant safeguards by emailing us.

If you are in Canada or Australia, note that information held in India is subject to lawful access requests by Indian authorities.

8. Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you, and receive a copy.
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your information, where we have no overriding legal reason to keep it.
  • Restrict or object to how we use it.
  • Port your information to another provider in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including consent to process the contents of your CV.

Email hello@trulyplaced.com and we will respond within 30 days. We will not charge you or treat you differently for exercising these rights.

One honest limitation: we cannot recall an application already submitted, or delete your CV from an employer's systems. Those requests must go to the employer directly, and we will help you identify who to contact.

If you are in the UK or EEA

You have the right to complain to a supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the chance to resolve it with you first.

If you are in Canada

Our handling of your information is intended to comply with PIPEDA. You may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

If you are in Australia

You may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner under the Privacy Act 1988.

If you are in California

You have the rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale or sharing under the CCPA as amended. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined.

9. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit and at rest, access restricted to team members who need it, and credentials stored in an encrypted vault rather than in documents or messages. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — but if a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant regulator without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours where the law requires it.
