    FAQs

    Need assistance?

    Our team is on WhatsApp and usually replies instantly. Or open the app and see the jobs that fit you.

    Try it freeChat with us
    Does TrulyPlaced apply without asking me?+
    Only within what you approve. You first sign off on your rebuilt resume and the roles, locations, salary and companies you want; after that, your specialist applies on your behalf every business day. Every application shows up in your tracker with the exact resume and cover letter that was sent.
    Where do the jobs come from?+
    Straight from employers' own hiring pages on platforms like Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workable, so we usually apply within 1–4 days of a role being posted. Each posting is checked for scams, seniority and sponsorship before anything is sent.
    Will my resume still sound like me?+
    Yes. Your specialist rewrites the wording to match each job, never the facts: your roles, dates, numbers and skills stay exactly as you gave them. You review and approve the rebuilt resume before a single application goes out.
    What happens after I apply?+
    Every application lands in your tracker with its status. Employer replies and interview invites arrive in your TrulyPlaced inbox and you get an alert. Your specialist watches where the callbacks come from and adjusts the next batch.
    Can I cancel any time?+
    Yes. There's no subscription: you pay once for a plan. If you stop early, a small one-time onboarding fee is kept and the rest is refunded for the applications we haven't sent yet. The full method is on our Cancellation & Refund Policy page.
