Your data, protected

Your resume and logins stay yours

You're trusting us with your resume, your work-authorisation details and, if you choose, access to job boards in your name. We treat that access the way you would.

Used only to apply — nothing else

When you give us job-board access, we sign in only to submit the applications you've authorised. We never read your personal messages, change your profile, or use your login for anything beyond applying — and we sign out the moment the work is done.

Accessed only during active work

We open your account to apply, then log out for the rest of the day. There is no standing, always-on connection to your logins — access happens in a short, deliberate session and ends with it.

Encrypted and need-to-know

Any credentials you share are stored encrypted, never in plain documents or chat. Only the recruiter assigned to your search can see your details, and everyone on our team is bound by confidentiality.

You can revoke access instantly

It's your account. Change your password at any time and our access ends immediately — no request to us required. Tell us and we'll confirm it and delete anything we held. We never sell or share your data with advertisers.

Is there a refund policy if I'm not satisfied with the service?+
Yes. Each plan includes a one-time, non-refundable Platform & onboarding fee of $100 that covers activating your account, your portal and your rewritten resume. Beyond that, the rest of your plan is refunded pro-rata for the applications we haven't yet submitted — so you only pay for the work you've actually received. The full method, with a worked example, is on our Cancellation & Refund Policy page.
What if I don't receive any interview calls?+
We don't guarantee interviews — no honest service can, because hiring decisions sit with employers, not us. What we commit to is the work: a properly built resume and a sustained volume of well-targeted applications every business day. If responses are low partway through your plan, your recruiter will rework your resume and retarget your roles at no extra cost — adjusting the approach is part of the service, not an add-on.
Why should I pay when I can apply to jobs on my own?+
You can absolutely apply on your own. What you're paying for is time and consistency: a recruiter rewrites your resume, finds roles within days of posting, tailors every application, submits 25–30 each business day, and tracks it all in one portal — so your search keeps moving while you focus on interviews and networking.
What is TrulyPlaced's success rate?+
We don't publish a single success-rate figure, because outcomes depend on your field, experience and the market — any one number would be misleading. What we can show you is exactly what we do: 25–30 tailored applications every business day, roles sourced within 1–4 days of posting, and a resume built and reworked by an experienced recruiter. We'd rather be judged on the work we control than on a statistic we can't.
How quickly can I expect an interview call?+
We can't promise a timeline — interview calls depend on employers and the market. What we can promise is that applications begin within two business days of your resume approval, at 25–30 per business day, so your search is active fast. When responses come, they typically arrive a few weeks into a sprint.
How does TrulyPlaced compare to other job-search services?+
We pair experienced recruiters with a repeatable process: resumes built to FAANG standards and reworked on your feedback, roles found within days of posting, every application tailored to the job description, and everything tracked in one transparent portal — so you always see exactly what's been done.
Can I track my job applications and progress?+
Absolutely. Real-time tracking lets you monitor every application we submit on your behalf, along with your interview schedule — all from one easy-to-use client portal.
Can I tell my associate to apply for specific jobs?+
Yes. Through the client portal you can delegate in one click — flag specific listings or leave instructions, so your preferences are built right into every application we send.
