About TrulyPlaced

TrulyPlaced (sometimes written Truly Placed) is a brand of RepoReserve Technologies Private Limited

TrulyPlaced is a done-for-you job application service. A real career specialist rewrites your resume, finds roles that fit you within days of them being posted, and applies to 25–30 of them for you every business day, tailoring each application to the job. You follow every application and interview in your own client portal, and spend your time on the interviews instead of the forms.

What we do

  • Your resume, rebuilt. An experienced career specialist rewrites it for the roles you want, and reworks it on your feedback.
  • Roles found early. We look for jobs that match your experience, location and salary within 1–4 days of posting, when they're still open to new applicants.
  • Applications sent for you. 25–30 tailored applications every business day, each one matched to the job description.
  • Everything visible. Every application, its status and your interviews in one client portal.

We serve job seekers in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia. Plans are a one-time payment for a set number of applications, with no subscription. See the home page for how a sprint works and our plans, and our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

TrulyPlaced Autopilot

For people who'd rather do it themselves, TrulyPlaced Autopilot is our app: it finds roles that fit your resume, tailors your resume and answers for each one, and applies for you. It's free to start. You can also browse open jobs without an account.

Free tools

Resume X-Ray is a free ATS resume checker: it shows what an applicant tracking system reads from your resume and what it drops, grades every bullet, and checks your match against a job. It runs in your browser; your resume never leaves your device.
