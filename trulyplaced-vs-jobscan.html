How TrulyPlaced Autopilot works

  1. Upload your resume. It's read for you and your profile fills itself in: experience, skills, contact details. You only check it.
  2. Say what you want. The roles, the cities or remote, and the answers every application asks (work authorization, sponsorship, where you live). Once, not on every form.
  3. Watch your matches arrive. In about 20 seconds you see real US openings scored against your profile, like "91% match". New postings come in every day; closed ones are taken off, and postings that look fake are flagged and skipped.
  4. Autopilot applies, every day. It picks your best new matches, rewrites your resume's summary and bullets for each job and writes a cover letter, then fills in the employer's own application page and submits it. You set how many a day (up to 25) and how good a match has to be. Want more? Send any job with one tap, or choose Review first to check it before it goes.
  5. Replies come to one inbox. Every application uses your own TrulyPlaced email address. When an employer emails a verification code, it's typed in for you. Interview invites and replies are sorted, and you get an alert when something needs you.
  6. Everything is on record. Each application shows the exact resume and cover letter sent and a screenshot of the employer's confirmation page, so you always know what went out in your name.

Also included

  • Review first, if you like. Let Autopilot send on its own, or have each application prepared for you to check and approve before anything goes out.
  • Answer once, never again. Every answer you give is remembered and reused when another form asks the same thing, even in different words.
  • Matches that learn. Pass on a job and say why ("not my field", "too senior"), and that kind of job stops coming back.
  • Any job, by link. Paste a Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or Workable job link and it's added to your list, ready to apply. Or search every open job in plain words.
  • No wasted credits. Right before sending, the app checks the job is still open. If an application can't be sent, the credit comes back.
  • Interviews, made easy. Interview and assessment emails get Join and Schedule buttons, the deadline and Add to calendar, plus an instant alert.
  • Your tracker updates itself. Employer emails move each application to Interview, Offer or Rejected for you.
  • A nudge to follow up. If an employer hasn't replied after a week, you're reminded to follow up.
  • A real resume editor. Keep several resumes, pick a template, download a PDF, and see every tailored version that was sent.
  • Your own Gmail, if you prefer. Use your TrulyPlaced address, or connect Gmail and codes are read from it automatically.
  • Your data stays yours. It's used only for your applications, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

What it won't do

  • Make things up. Tailoring changes the wording, never your facts: no invented jobs, titles, dates or skills.
  • Guess an answer. If a form asks something you haven't answered, it stops and asks you.
  • Get around a site's security checks. If an employer's site asks you to prove you're human, it hands that step to you.
  • Lock you in. Monthly, cancel any time, and the first 25 applications are free.

Usually not. Autopilot tailors your resume to each job description before it applies. If you like detailed keyword scores, TrulyPlaced also has a free resume checker, Resume X-Ray.

Yes. Autopilot Pro is $19.99 a month; independent reviews list Jobscan Premium at $49.95 a month.

Does Jobscan apply to jobs?
